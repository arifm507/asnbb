Uttar Pradesh is often called the **heartland of India** — and for good reason. For thousands of years, its plains along the Ganga and Yamuna have been the stage for some of the subcontinent's most important events.

## Ancient roots

The region was home to the great **Mahajanapadas** of Kosala and Kashi. Cities like **Varanasi (Kashi)** are among the oldest continuously inhabited places on earth. It was in UP, at **Sarnath**, that the Buddha delivered his first sermon.

## Medieval grandeur

Through the medieval period, the land saw the rise of powerful dynasties and the flowering of a shared culture. Under the **Mughals**, cities such as **Agra** and **Fatehpur Sikri** became centres of art, architecture, and administration — the **Taj Mahal** stands as the era's crowning achievement.

## The first spark of freedom

Uttar Pradesh played a central role in India's struggle for independence. The **Revolt of 1857** erupted across **Meerut, Kanpur, Jhansi, and Lucknow**, with leaders like **Rani Lakshmibai** becoming immortal symbols of resistance.

## A cultural treasure house

UP's contribution to Indian culture is immense:

- **Music:** the Lucknow and Banaras gharanas.
- **Literature:** poets and writers in Hindi, Urdu, and Awadhi.
- **Festivals:** from the grand **Kumbh Mela** at Prayagraj to Diwali in Ayodhya.
